ABOUT US

A Comfortable Stay in Adilabad.

Hotel Ravi Teja is a well-established hospitality property located on National Highway 7, near the main Bus Stand in Adilabad, Telangana. Our central location makes us the ideal choice for travellers, business guests, and families visiting the region.

We believe in delivering genuine comfort at honest prices. Whether you are here for a quick transit stay or an extended visit, our team is committed to making your experience warm, comfortable, and memorable. From our neatly maintained rooms to our round-the-clock front desk service, every aspect of your stay is handled with care.
